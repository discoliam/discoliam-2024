<p class="Lead">I'm a freelance headless CMS and Jamstack developer based in Bristol, UK. I build fast, secure websites that combine a CMS your team loves with a modern front end your visitors will love.</p>

A headless website separates where your content lives from how it's shown. Your team edits content in a CMS like WordPress, Craft CMS or Sanity, and the front end is built separately with a modern framework, then pre-rendered and served from a global network. The result is a website that's faster, more secure and much more flexible.

## Why go headless?

- **Speed:** pages are pre-built and served from the edge, so they load almost instantly.
- **Security:** with no public-facing CMS or database, there's far less to attack.
- **Flexibility:** the same content can power your website, apps, microsites and more.
- **Lower environmental impact:** serving static files uses far less energy than rendering every page on a server.
- **Keep your CMS:** your editors can keep using the CMS they already know.
