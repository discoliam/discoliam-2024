<p class="Lead">I'm a freelance Shopify developer based in Bristol, UK. I build custom Shopify themes and ecommerce front ends that are fast, accessible and easy for your team to manage.</p>

[Shopify](https://www.shopify.com/) is a brilliant platform for selling online, but many stores end up on a heavily customised off-the-shelf theme, weighed down by apps and scripts. A custom theme built around your products and customers can make your store faster, easier to use and easier to grow.

## What I can help with

### Custom Shopify themes

Bespoke themes built in Liquid from the ground up, using Shopify's sections and blocks so your team can build and rearrange pages without a developer. For [Bluum](/portfolio/bluum/), I built a fully custom theme that shows off their green-roofed garden storage, with interactive product galleries and options built with [Alpine.js](https://alpinejs.dev/).

### Theme rebuilds

Outgrown your current theme? I can rebuild it around how your business actually works. For [Bower Collective](/portfolio/bower-collective/), I worked with their CTO to replace a customised off-the-shelf theme with a brand new build, improving both performance and accessibility scores.

### Custom customer experiences

Subscriptions, customer accounts and order management often need more than a theme can offer. I built a library of [Vue 3](https://vuejs.org/) components, documented in [Storybook](https://storybook.js.org/), to power Bower Collective's fully custom customer portal, making subscription and order management much smoother for their customers.

### App integrations

Reviews, subscriptions, search and marketing tools all need to fit into your store without slowing it down. I integrate third-party apps carefully, so they look like part of your brand and don't drag down page speed.

### Performance and accessibility

A faster store sells more, and an accessible store sells to everyone. I can audit an existing Shopify store and fix the issues, from bloated apps and unoptimised images to keyboard traps and missing labels. See my [technical audits](/services/technical-audits/) for more.

## How I work

Every project is different, but my Shopify builds usually include:

- A custom theme built with sections and blocks, designed with your team.
- Accessibility built in from the start, not bolted on at the end.
- Performance budgets, optimised images and minimal JavaScript.
- Careful, considered app integrations.
- Version-controlled theme code with a proper development and preview workflow.
- Handover documentation and training, so your team is confident managing the store.
