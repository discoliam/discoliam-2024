## Services and technology

### Which CMS should I use?

It depends on your content, your team and your budget. [WordPress](/services/wordpress-development/) is familiar and flexible, [Craft CMS](/services/craft-cms-development/) is brilliant for structured, content-rich sites, and [Shopify](/services/shopify-development/) is hard to beat for ecommerce. I'm happy to talk through the options and recommend the best fit.

### What is a headless website, and do I need one?

A headless website separates your CMS from the front end, so pages can be pre-built and served from the edge, making them faster and more secure. It's a great fit for some projects but not every one. My [headless development](/services/headless-development/) page explains more.

### Can you work on my existing website?

Yes. I often take over existing sites to fix bugs, add features, improve performance or upgrade CMS versions. I'll usually start with a short review of the codebase so we both know what we're working with.

### Do you design websites too?

My focus is front-end development, but I work closely with designers and can help with UX, wireframes and prototyping. If you need a full design, I can recommend designers and agencies I trust. [Adjust to suit what you offer.]

### Will my website be accessible?

Yes. Accessibility is built into everything I make, not added at the end. I aim for WCAG AA as standard, testing with keyboards, screen readers and automated tools. I also offer [accessibility audits](/services/technical-audits/) for existing sites.

### Will my website be fast and good for SEO?

Performance and technical SEO are part of every build: optimised images, minimal JavaScript, clean semantic markup, structured data and sensible page titles and descriptions. I can also [audit an existing site](/services/technical-audits/) and fix what's holding it back.

### What do you mean by environmental impact?

Every website uses energy to load. By keeping pages light, choosing efficient hosting and avoiding unnecessary scripts, I build sites with a lower carbon footprint, which usually makes them faster too.
