Bluum makes garden storage with living green roofs, along with a growing range of sustainable garden products. Their customers want to see exactly what they're buying, so the store needed to show off the products in their best light while making it easy to compare sizes, options and add-ons.

I built a fully custom Shopify theme, written in Liquid and organised around Shopify's sections and blocks, so the Bluum team can build and rearrange pages without needing a developer. Interactive pieces, like product galleries, option pickers and the cart, use [Alpine.js](https://alpinejs.dev/) and [Swiper](https://swiperjs.com/), keeping the JavaScript light and progressively enhanced.

I also integrated Yotpo reviews into product pages and collections, so social proof shows up right where customers are making decisions. Throughout the build I kept a close eye on performance and accessibility, which is no small thing on an image-heavy ecommerce site.
