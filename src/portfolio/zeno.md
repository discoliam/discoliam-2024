After a rebrand, Zeno needed a brand new website to support its goal of installing solar solutions in over 1 million homes by 2040. Working with Massive Media, I built a site that explains Zeno's products clearly and turns interest into enquiries.

The site is a custom WordPress theme built with Gutenberg and [ACF](https://www.advancedcustomfields.com/) blocks, so the Zeno team can create and update product pages, guides and campaigns as their offering grows. The new brand is carried through a flexible set of components that keep every page consistent.

For a company focused on sustainability, the website needed to be efficient too. I kept the front end lean, with optimised images, minimal JavaScript and careful use of third-party scripts, so pages load quickly and use as little energy as possible.
