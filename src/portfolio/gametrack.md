GameTrack is a suite of apps for macOS, iOS and iPadOS that helps players keep track of their games. They first asked me to help add new features and improve performance on their website. But after auditing the existing codebase, it was clear that a rebuild would give them a much better foundation than patching what was there.

I rebuilt the site in [Vue 3](https://vuejs.org/), replacing an ageing UI library with [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/). This made the codebase much smaller, faster and easier to maintain. I also worked on the UX and UI of key pages, making sure the website feels as polished as the apps it represents.

Deploying to [Netlify](https://www.netlify.com/) gave the team preview builds for every change and simple, reliable deploys. With a clean component structure in place, adding new features to complement the apps is now quick and low-risk.
