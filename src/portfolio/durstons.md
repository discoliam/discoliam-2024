Durstons has been making compost and garden products for UK garden centres and retailers for decades. With their growing range of peat-free composts, barks and soil conditioners growing, they needed a website that helps gardeners find the right product and shows off the brand's growing commitment to peat-free gardening.

Working closey with Digital and Design Directors at [FutureKings](https://futurekings.co.uk), I built a custom WordPress theme around a library of reusable blocks, so the Durstons team can put together product pages, guides and landing pages themselves. Products are organised into clear categories and use cases, so it's easy to answer questions like "which compost for seeds?" without wading through technical spec sheets.

As with all my builds, I focused on performance and accessibility from the start. That means responsive, optimised imagery, semantic markup and keyboard-friendly navigation, so the site works well for everyone, whether they're in the garden on a phone or in a garden centre office.
