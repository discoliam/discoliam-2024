<p class="Lead">I'm a freelance front-end & web developer based in Bristol, UK, working with clients across the UK and around the world. Here's a brief overview of what I do, how I go about it, and the type of organisations I work with.</p>

## How I can help

- **[WordPress development](/services/wordpress-development/):** custom themes, Gutenberg and ACF blocks, and headless WordPress.
- **[Craft CMS development](/services/craft-cms-development/):** bespoke Craft builds, both traditional and headless.
- **[Shopify development](/services/shopify-development/):** custom themes, theme rebuilds and customer portals.
- **[Headless and Jamstack development](/services/headless-development/):** fast, pre-rendered front ends with Astro, Next.js and Eleventy.
- **[Technical audits](/services/technical-audits/):** performance, accessibility, SEO and environmental impact reviews, with clear fixes.

## What I do

My work covers all kinds of digital products and services, and I'm often involved at several stages of a project.

### UX and strategy

I love getting involved in the nitty-gritty of user research, workshops and UX design. I work closely with UI designers and brand managers to turn static assets and ideas into interactive, accessible digital products.

### Front-end development

Whether it's planning the technical architecture of a small website, or parachuting in to add new features to a legacy codebase, I've worked on all sorts of projects. I specialise in front-end development, across a wide range of tech stacks and CMSs.

I try to focus on the **accessibility**, **performance** and **environmental impact** of all my work.

### Consultancy

Sometimes clients just need some help or guidance with their digital presence. Whether it's a [technical audit](/services/technical-audits/) with clear recommendations, advice on technical architecture, or a full website review, I love getting stuck in, investigating the nuts and bolts and working out how we can improve things.

## How I work

Whether your team is big, small or non-existent, I'm happy working with whoever is on board. I think collaboration is the key to successful projects, whether that's with other developers, designers, CEOs or any other project stakeholders.

Once we're up and running, I like to choose the best tool for the job, and one that the rest of the team is comfortable with. But generally speaking, I find I'm often:

- Wireframing and prototyping in [Figma](https://www.figma.com/).
- Building proofs of concept with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, or in [Webflow](https://webflow.com/).
- Creating [Jamstack](https://jamstack.org/) websites from scratch, using tools like [Astro](https://astro.build/), [Eleventy](https://www.11ty.dev/) and [Sanity](https://www.sanity.io/), or headless WordPress and Craft CMS.
- Working with [WordPress](https://wordpress.org/) or [Craft CMS](https://craftcms.com/) on more traditional server-rendered builds.
- Building modern single-page apps with [Vue](https://vuejs.org/).
- Crafting design systems in [Storybook](https://storybook.js.org/).

## Who I like to work with

My clients range from small one-person start-ups to international corporations, and everything in between. I like to work with clients who want to make the world a better place, however they can. This includes charities and NGOs, political activists, environmentally friendly retailers, alternative transport and energy providers, arts organisations, science and education, and public sector services.

Recent examples include [Ticket to Antarctica](/portfolio/ticket-to-antarctica/) for the British Antarctic Survey, nonprofit newsroom [Sentient Media](/portfolio/sentient-media/), and [Bristol Good Food 2030](/portfolio/bristol-good-food/). You can see more in my [portfolio](/portfolio/).
